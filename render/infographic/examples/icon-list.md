title: The Theme Contract

:: icon-list
headline: 10 keys that decide **how it looks**
sub: Nine frozen keys plus one optional logo. No other value in the engine is a brand value.
items:
- bg | The canvas ground. Every surface and rule steps from here.
- fg | Primary ink. Secondary text and hairlines are mixed from it.
- accent | The one hue. Numbers, bars, rings, ticks, leader lines.
- muted | Captions and meta. The only de-emphasized text colour.
- font_head | Display stack. Headline, labels, numerals, section titles.
- font_body | Body stack. Prose, descriptions, card bodies, details.
- scale | Dense or airy. One multiplier over padding and gaps.
- radius | Corner radius on every rectangle. Zero means square.
- rule_weight | The hairline. Structural borders derive as multiples.
- logo | Optional. A path inside the profile, or no mark at all.
