---
title: The Seven Layouts
---

:: card-grid
headline: The seven layouts this **engine draws**
sub: Each takes a different shape of material. The material picks the layout, never the topic.
items:
- Numbered steps | Ordered stages of one process, rings alternating down a single serpentine connector.
- Card grid | A flat set of peers, one numbered card each, rows stretching to fill the frame.
- Comparison panel | Two or three labels answered on the same rows, with a shared-ground verdict band.
- Icon list | A longer flat set, two columns, lighter than cards and reading as one continuous list.
- Funnel | Interlocking trapezoid bands narrowing top to bottom, one hue stepped through luminances.
- Hybrid playbook | A band of large numbers over a grid of titled sections, for mixed-density material.
- Annotated diagram | One central node with callouts joined to its rim by thin leader lines.
