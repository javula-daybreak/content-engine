Digital transformation is not a technology initiative.

It is an organizational change initiative that happens to involve technology. The distinction matters because it determines who owns the outcome, and in most enterprises the ownership sits with a function that does not control the behavior that has to change.

Northwind learned this in 2024 when it completed a platform migration on schedule and on budget, and then discovered that the teams it was built for had continued to use the previous system because nobody had changed the incentive that made the old workflow faster for them.
