# Audience

Who this person is writing for, and specifically what those people believe that
this person thinks is wrong. That third term is what supplies a counterparty:
inventory item crossed with thesis alone can only produce illustrations.

The two vocabulary fields, added 2026-08-23, are read by `reference/reader.md`'s
context block at pipeline step 5b. Register mismatch — aiming one altitude's
vocabulary at another — is the sharpest thing that grader catches, and until
these existed no file in the repo held a slot for it.

Staleness interval: 120 days.

```
who:
their_job:
what_they_believe_that_is_wrong:
  - <one per line>
what_they_are_tired_of_hearing:
  - <one per line>
their_words:
  - <one per line, the words these people actually use for this>
words_they_never_use:
  - <one per line, the words that would tell them you are not one of them>
source: interview | pasted | derived | proposed
last_reviewed:
```
