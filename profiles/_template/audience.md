# Audience

Who this person is writing for, and specifically what those people believe that
this person thinks is wrong. That third term is what supplies a counterparty:
inventory item crossed with thesis alone can only produce illustrations.

Staleness interval: 120 days.

```

their_job:
what_they_believe_that_is_wrong:
  - <one per line>
what_they_are_tired_of_hearing:
  - <one per line>
source: interview | pasted | derived | proposed
last_reviewed:
```
