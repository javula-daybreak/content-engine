# Audience

Who this person is writing for, and specifically what those people believe that
this person thinks is wrong. That third term is what supplies a counterparty:
inventory item crossed with thesis alone can only produce illustrations.

The two vocabulary fields, added 2026-08-23, are read by `reference/reader.md`'s
context block at pipeline step 5b. Register mismatch — aiming one altitude's
vocabulary at another — is the sharpest thing that grader catches, and until
these existed no file in the repo held a slot for it.

Staleness interval: 120 days.

```
who:
their_job:
what_they_believe_that_is_wrong:
  - <one per line>
what_they_are_tired_of_hearing:
  - <one per line>
their_words:
  - <one per line, the words these people actually use for this>
words_they_never_use:
  - <one per line, the words that would tell them you are not one of them>
source: interview | pasted | derived | proposed
last_reviewed:
```

## Audience

```
who: hospital COOs and VPs of surgical services; OR managers second
their_job: they own surgical margin and they are measured on case volume and on
           labor cost per case
what_they_believe_that_is_wrong:
  - OR throughput is a speed problem: flip the room faster, start on time, get
    more cases
  - the last twenty minutes inside the room is where the capacity is
what_they_are_tired_of_hearing:
  - that Lean or Six Sigma will fix the OR
  - that what they need is a better dashboard
  - that on-time starts are a culture problem
  - benchmark percentiles from a peer group they have never seen
their_words:
  - block time
  - block utilization
  - prime time
  - first case on-time start
  - turnover
  - PAT
  - add-on
  - flip the room
  - wheels in, wheels out
  - release
  - case mix
words_they_never_use:
  - patient journey
  - surgical ecosystem
  - throughput optimization
  - care delivery transformation
  - operating theatre
  - OR utilization (a consultant says this; we say block or prime-time utilization)
  - leverage
source: interview
last_reviewed:
```
