41 postmortems last year: 19 action items, 3 shipped.

I went back through them in March, because I wanted to prove the process was working. It was not.

The blameless part worked, and it worked well: nobody got blamed, nobody named a person, nobody got put on a plan, and every one of those meetings ended on time.

Then the action items went into a backlog owned by whoever had the fewest objections. The person who would have had to fund the work was not in the room. Never was. We invite the people who were there for the outage, which is, exactly, the set of people who cannot authorise the fix.

So we ran 41 of them, and produced a document trail.

Blameless was never the hard part. A review with no budget attached is a review that changes nothing.

3 out of 19.
